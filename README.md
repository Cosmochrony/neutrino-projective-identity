# Neutrino Mixing and Projective Particle Identity

A structural companion note to the fermionic-matter sub-programme of the Cosmochrony research programme.

## Abstract

Neutrino oscillations establish that the weak flavour basis and the mass propagation basis of the lepton sector
are not aligned.
This note examines what this misalignment means for the ontology of particle identity within the Cosmochrony
framework, where particles are invariants of a non-injective projection rather than primitive objects.
Basis misalignment is argued to be the generic situation (neutral meson systems exhibit the same structure, the
neutrino being distinguished only by its elementarity), while basis alignment requires an identity-stabilising
projection channel.
Charged fermions possess such channels; neutrinos do not.
The large PMNS mixing is then the unfixed default, consistent with the anarchy hypothesis, while the
near-diagonality of the CKM matrix becomes the non-trivial explanandum, conditional on a common projective frame
assumption.
The Dirac-or-Majorana alternative is reread as the degree to which the projection resolves relational
conjugation.
The note is structural throughout: it derives no mixing angles and no masses, and retains the standard
oscillation formalism as the operational effective description.
